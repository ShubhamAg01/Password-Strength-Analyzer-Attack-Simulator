Cryptographic analysis

Unsalted digests (MD5, SHA-1) execute at hundreds of thousands of operations per second on a single CPU core, enabling rapid dictionary exhaustion. bcrypt introduces an adaptive cost schedule (2cost rounds) and per-user 128-bit random salt, enforcing computational delays that neutralize offline wordlists.
